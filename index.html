The Series

The Bible is the best-selling book in history, with more than 5 billion copies sold. GENESIS is a multi-season premium series that brings foundational stories to life for contemporary audiences. Its first season spans 8 episodes, each following a different central character, turning universally known biblical narratives into cinematic, character-driven drama.

I Episode 01 · Noah

Noah and the Flood

As the world collapses into violence and corruption, Noah receives an impossible command: build an ark before a catastrophe no one believes is coming.

Mocked and isolated, he gathers his family and the animals as the skies finally open and the familiar world disappears beneath the water.

The ark, the flood and the rainbow become some of the Bible’s most enduring images: a story of destruction, survival and a second beginning.

II Episode 02 · Abraham

Abraham and the Binding of Isaac

After a lifetime waiting for the son God promised him, Abraham is given an unbearable command: take Isaac to the mountain and offer him back.

Father and son climb together toward a moment where faith, love and obedience collide, until divine intervention changes everything at the final instant.

The Binding of Isaac becomes one of the Bible’s most powerful and unsettling tests of faith, built around an unforgettable father-and-son image.

III Episode 03 · Joseph

Joseph and His Brothers

Joseph, the favored son and dreamer, becomes the target of his brothers’ jealousy and is betrayed, sold into slavery and carried away to Egypt.

What begins as a family crime becomes an astonishing reversal as Joseph rises from slave and prisoner to one of the most powerful men in the kingdom.

Years later, famine brings his brothers before him, forcing Joseph to choose between revenge and forgiveness without revealing who he is.

IV Episode 04 · Moses

Moses and the Exodus

A Hebrew baby marked for death is saved from the Nile and raised inside Pharaoh’s palace, only to return years later as the liberator of his own people.

Moses challenges the most powerful ruler on earth, and Egypt is shaken by the plagues until Pharaoh finally allows the enslaved Israelites to leave.

With the Egyptian army closing in and the sea blocking their escape, the Exodus reaches its iconic climax in the crossing of the Red Sea.

V Episode 05 · Joshua

Joshua and Jericho

After Moses’ death, Joshua inherits the impossible task of leading a new generation into the Promised Land.

His first great obstacle is Jericho, a fortified city that cannot be taken by conventional force, leading to a strange divine strategy of marching, silence and trumpets.

On the seventh day, the horns sound and the walls collapse, creating one of the Bible’s most spectacular images of faith becoming action.

VI Episode 06 · Samson

Samson and Delilah

Samson is a man of extraordinary strength, feared by his enemies and bound from birth to a sacred secret: his uncut hair.

The Philistines discover that the one person capable of defeating him is Delilah, the woman he loves and trusts enough to reveal the source of his power.

Betrayed and stripped of his strength, Samson falls from invincible hero to captive, before one final act turns his downfall into legend.

VII Episode 07 · David

David and Goliath

An unknown young shepherd arrives at a battlefield where an entire army has been paralyzed by the giant Philistine champion Goliath.

Rejecting armor and conventional weapons, David walks out alone with a sling, five stones and a confidence no one around him understands.

One impossible shot transforms the shepherd into a national hero, and creates perhaps the Bible’s ultimate image of the underdog defeating the giant.

VIII Episode 08 · Solomon

King Solomon

David’s son inherits a fragile kingdom and asks God for wisdom rather than power, beginning an era of extraordinary prosperity and influence.

His legendary judgments, the building of the Temple and the arrival of the Queen of Sheba establish Solomon as a king whose wisdom is known far beyond Israel.

But behind the gold and grandeur lies a deeper story about the seduction of wealth and power, and the seeds of a kingdom that will not remain united.

The Creators

“

As Jewish actors and creators who grew up in traditional homes, the stories of the Bible have always been part of our lives. We grew up with them, imagining what those moments must have looked and felt like: standing before a sea as it parted, or being David as he faced Goliath.

Today, technology gives us the chance to bring what we have imagined to the screen, so that a new generation can feel the same connection and wonder we felt growing up.

We bring a deeply personal connection to these stories, along with our experience as actors and creators. That experience helps us find the people within these extraordinary events: what they feared, what they believed, and how they found the courage to act.”

Shalom & Daniel Assayag

Director

Shalom Assayag

One of Israel’s most popular comedians and performers, Shalom Assayag has become a leading director and creator of hit Israeli film and television. He directed and starred in the box-office hit HaHillula (2023) and co-directed its sequel, HaHillula 2: The Wedding (2025).

He co-created and starred in the long-running series The 80s (Channel 2, five seasons) and The 90s (Channel 13), and co-created Patrick, which is set to receive a U.S. remake. As the lead of the crime drama Manayek, he won two Israeli Television Academy Awards for Best Actor.

Writer

Daniel Assayag

Daniel Assayag is the head writer behind the Assayag family’s biggest recent successes. After writing episodes for the final season of The 80s, he became head writer of its follow-up, The 90s, on Channel 13, while also starring in the series.

He went on to write both HaHillula feature films, the 2023 box-office hit and its 2025 sequel, HaHillula 2: The Wedding, bringing a personal, character-driven voice to stories built for broad audiences.
